Onions are among the easiest vegetables to grow, if you follow a few basic rules.

## Growing

- **Soil**: loose and well drained, in a sunny spot.
- **Sowing**: timing depends on the variety and the climate, so follow the packet instructions.
- **Water**: regular, without waterlogging.
- **Weeds**: keep them under control, because onions have shallow roots.

## Harvest

Harvest when the leaves turn yellow and fall over. The onions should then be left to dry in an airy place sheltered from rain, before storing them.

## Storage

Well-dried onions keep in a cool, dry and airy place, in crates or nets. Remove straight away any that start to go mouldy.
