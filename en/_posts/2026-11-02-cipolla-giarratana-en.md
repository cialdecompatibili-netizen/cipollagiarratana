The **Giarratana onion** takes its name from Giarratana, in the province of Ragusa, and is a Slow Food Presidium. It stands out for its size: the bulbs are very large and can exceed a kilo.

## The flavour

It is sweet and delicate, with little pungency. That is why it is enjoyed raw too.

## How to use it

- **Raw**, sliced in a salad, perhaps with tomato and oil.
- **Roasted**, whole or halved, with a drizzle of oil.
- **Stuffed**, with breadcrumbs and herbs.
- **Sweet and sour**, slowly cooked.

## How to store it

Keep it in a cool, dry and airy place, without piling the onions up. Once cut, it goes in the fridge and is eaten within a few days.
